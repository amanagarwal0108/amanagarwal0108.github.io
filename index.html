About

I build AI systems that make complex enterprise data actionable. Over 9+ years, I have progressed from hands-on data science delivery to leading AI/ML delivery across KPMG, Volvo Eicher, Floweraura, Decision Point Analytics, Publicis Sapient, and Fractal Analytics.

My work spans both AI and ML: multi-agent orchestration, retrieval, evaluation, and LLM observability alongside predictive modelling, forecasting, optimisation, and MLOps. I am equally comfortable defining the technology roadmap, authoring architecture decisions, and writing the code that makes a data product reliable.

The aim is practical AI: systems that are secure, measurable, useful to the people who rely on them, and robust enough to keep improving after launch.

How I work

I approach AI and ML as full product and operating models, not just models. That means starting with the business decision, designing the data and system architecture, setting evaluation criteria, and making safety, security, observability, and feedback loops part of the build from day one.

In enterprise AI programmes, I have authored architecture decision records and high-level designs, shaped build-versus-buy decisions and technology roadmaps, and implemented guardrails including prompt-injection defences, PII redaction, policy enforcement, isolated agent execution, and audit trails.

I also lead and mentor cross-functional teams, helping data scientists and engineers turn emerging AI capabilities into governed systems that can be trusted at scale.

Architecture principles

The practices I bring to production AI programmes.

  1. Start with the decision.Anchor AI work to the operating workflow and business outcome it must improve.
  2. Evaluate before scale.Define grounded, domain-specific quality measures before relying on a system in production.
  3. Build safety in.Design access control, guardrails, auditability, isolation, and human oversight from the first release.
  4. Operate in the open.Use observability, feedback loops, and clear ownership so the system can improve safely over time.
